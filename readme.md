# 🎄 KY Christmas Mod for Luanti (Minetest) 🎄

Bring holiday cheer and festive magic to your Luanti world with **KY Christmas**! This mod adds non-craftable decorative Christmas items and a gentle, spawnable reindeer mob to make your winter wonderlands festive and full of life.

---

## ✨ Features & Highlights

- **🎄 Festive Decorative Items**: Decorate your winter cabins and snow-covered villages with 7 high-quality static 3D models:
  - 🛷 **Sled** (`sled.obj`)
  - 🎁 **Present Box A** (`present-a-cube.obj`)
  - 🎁 **Present Box B** (`present-b-cube.obj`)
  - ☃️ **Snowman** (`snowman.obj`)
  - 🎩 **Snowman Hat** (`snowman-hat.obj`)
  - 🎄 **Decorated Christmas Tree** (`tree-decorated.obj`)
  - ❄️ **Decorated Snowy Christmas Tree** (`tree-decorated-snow.obj`)

- **🦌 Spawnable Reindeer Mob**:
  - Based on the popular `mobs_redo` API.
  - Features smooth GLB animations on a continuous timeline (keyframes specified in seconds).
  - Peaceful idle behavior: wanders around enjoying the festive scenery.
  - Realistic reaction: flees when struck/punched.
  - Easily placeable anywhere using its custom inventory icon egg item.

- **🚫 Non-Craftable Inventory Items**: Designed specifically for decorative/event use without cluttering survival crafting grids.

---

## 📦 Installation Instructions

1. Download or clone this repository into your Minetest/Luanti `mods/` directory.
2. Ensure the folder is named `ky_christmas`.
3. Make sure you have the `mobs` (`mobs_redo`) mod installed and enabled in your world dependencies.
4. Enable `KY Christmas` in your world settings and launch the game!

---

## 🕹️ Usage Guide

### Placing Christmas Decorations
- Access the items through the creative inventory or spawn them using commands (e.g., `/giveme ky_christmas:snowman`).
- Right-click on any solid surface to place your decorations.

### Spawning Reindeers
- Find the Reindeer spawn egg in your inventory or use `/giveme ky_christmas:reindeer`.
- Right-click on the ground to spawn a reindeer in your world.
